1. Overview

Dailymettle ("we", "us", "the app") is a habit and challenge tracker built for the 75 Hard Challenge. This policy explains what data we process, why, and the choices you have. The app is designed to work offline first — cloud features are optional and only active when you sign in.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

By default, your challenges, daily progress, streaks, journal notes, task notes, and photos are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. If you never sign in, this data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. Account & Sign-In

If you enable cloud backup or the accountability-partner features, you sign in with Google or email. We process your account identifier (UID), email address, display name, and profile photo to authenticate you and to enable partner features. Authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication (Google).

4. Encrypted Cloud Backup (Optional)

When you enable cloud backup, your challenge sessions, daily progress, journal notes, task notes, and regular tasks are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption before being uploaded to Firebase Firestore. They are stored there only as encrypted content.

  • The encryption key is derived from your account identifier (using HKDF-SHA256) so that only your account can restore and decrypt your backup after a reinstall — this supports restore, access, and accountability.
  • Access to your backup is additionally restricted to your account by Firebase security rules.
  • A small amount of data is stored in readable form by design so cross-user and lookup features work: your profile (name, email, photo), aggregate progress counts (no task details), and partnership records.

If you do not enable backup, no progress data leaves your device.

5. Accountability Partners (Optional)

If you invite or accept a partner, limited information is shared to make the feature work: your display name, aggregate progress counts, the task names you choose to share, and any photo proof you submit for review. Partners can approve or reject the tasks you assign to each other. Please do not include sensitive personal information in task names, notes, or photos.

6. Photos

When you attach a photo as task proof or a custom task icon, the image is taken from your camera or photo library with your permission. Photos attached to accountability tasks are uploaded to our media hosting provider (Cloudinary) so your partner can review them. These photos are not encrypted; please avoid including sensitive information in them.

7. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand aggregate usage patterns and diagnose crashes so we can improve stability. This may include app opens, feature usage, task and challenge activity, device model, and crash diagnostics. We do not use this data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

8. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local reminders and may receive push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging to remind you about your tasks and partner activity. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

9. Permissions We Request

  • Notifications — to send reminders.
  • Camera & Photo Library — to attach photo proof or custom task icons (optional).
  • Internet — for cloud backup, partner features, notifications, and motivational quotes.

Each permission is used only for its stated purpose, and the photo/camera permissions are only requested when you use those features.

10. Third-Party Services

We rely on:

  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging (Google)
  • Cloudinary for hosting photos you attach to tasks
  • A public quotes API for motivational quotes

Google processes data under its own privacy policy. See policies.google.com/privacy.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. If you used cloud backup, you can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data by contacting us at the address below; we will delete your backup and account records.

12. Your Rights

  • Use the app fully offline without signing in.
  • Export your data as JSON from Settings.
  • Disable notifications and photo permissions in device settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your cloud data.

13. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here and in the app with an updated date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact us at dailymettle@gmail.com.
