Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 75 Hard Challenge?
75 Hard is a mental toughness program where you complete 5 tasks every day for 75 consecutive days:
  1. Two 45-minute workouts (one must be outdoors)
  2. Follow a diet with no cheat meals
  3. Drink 1 gallon of water
  4. Read 10 pages of a non-fiction book
  5. Take a progress photo
Miss a single task and you start over from Day 1. Dailymettle helps you track all five every day.
Do I need an internet connection to use the app?
No. Dailymettle is offline-first. All your progress, streaks, and photos are stored locally on your device, so you can track your challenge anywhere — no account or connection required.
How do I set up daily reminders?
Open the app, go to Settings → Reminders, and enable notifications for each task. You can customize the time for each reminder. Make sure notifications are allowed for Dailymettle in your device's system settings.
My progress or streak disappeared. What happened?
Because Dailymettle stores data locally on your device, progress can be lost if the app is uninstalled or the device is reset. To avoid this:
  • Avoid uninstalling the app mid-challenge
  • Keep your device's storage from being cleared for the app
If you believe this is a bug, email us with your device details and we'll investigate.
How do I delete my data?
All data is stored only on your device. You can reset your progress from within the app's settings, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. We do not collect or store your personal data on any server.
Is the app free? Are there ads or subscriptions?
Yes, Dailymettle is completely free. There are no ads and no subscriptions.
I'm having trouble on my iPhone/iPad. What should I do?
Try these steps first:
  1. Make sure you're running the latest version of the app from the App Store.
  2. Restart the app fully (swipe it away from the app switcher and reopen).
  3. Confirm your iOS version is up to date.
  4. Restart your device.
If the problem continues, email dailymettle@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and app version.
